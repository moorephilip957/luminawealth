Our Story

Born from a Vision to Transform Finance

LuminaWealthAI was founded in 2023 by a team of AI researchers, quantitative traders, and financial experts who saw a gap in the market: sophisticated AI trading tools were only available to institutional investors with millions in capital.

We believed that everyone deserves access to the same powerful AI technology that hedge funds use. So we built a platform that brings institutional-grade AI trading to retail investors — starting with just $50.

Today, LuminaWealthAI manages over $2.4 million in assets for 1,234+ investors worldwide, with our AI bots executing thousands of trades daily with a 94.7% accuracy rate.
