At LuminaWealthAI, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our AI-powered investment platform.

1. Introduction

LuminaWealthAI ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy and personal data. This Privacy Policy describes:
  • What information we collect and why
  • How we use your information
  • Who we share your information with
  • How we protect your information
  • Your rights regarding your information
GDPR Compliance: We comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable data protection laws. If you are a European Union resident, you have additional rights under GDPR.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

We collect the following personal information when you create an account:
Category Examples Purpose
Identity Data Full name, date of birth, nationality Account creation, KYC verification
Contact Data Email address, phone number, residential address Communication, account recovery

2.2 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from:
  • KYC verification providers: Identity verification services
  • Payment processors: Transaction and payment information
  • Blockchain networks: Cryptocurrency transaction data
  • Regulatory authorities: Information required for compliance

We do not collect:
  • Your private keys or seed phrases (you maintain full control)
  • Sensitive biometric data (except for optional facial recognition for 2FA)
  • Health information, religious beliefs, or political opinions
  • Information about children under 18

  • Send promotional emails and newsletters (with your consent)

Legal Basis for Processing: We process your data based on: (1) contract performance (to provide our services), (2) legal obligations (KYC/AML compliance), (3) legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention), and (4) your consent (marketing communications).

We may disclose your information when required by:

If LuminaWealthAI is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the acquiring entity. We will notify you of any such change.

4.4 With Your Consent

We may share your information with third parties when you explicitly consent to such sharing.
Important: We never share your private keys, passwords, or 2FA codes with anyone. You maintain full control of your cryptographic keys.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-leading security measures to protect your information:
  • 256-bit SSL encryption: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted
  • Cold storage: 95% of cryptocurrency assets are stored in offline cold storage
  • Multi-signature wallets: Require multiple approvals for large transactions
  • Two-factor authentication (2FA): Mandatory for all accounts
  • AI fraud detection: Real-time monitoring for suspicious activity
  • Regular security audits: Quarterly third-party penetration testing
  • Employee access controls: Strict access controls and background checks
  • Data encryption at rest: All stored data is encrypted
Security Certifications: We are SOC 2 Type II certified, ISO 27001 compliant, and PCI DSS Level 1 compliant. Our security practices meet or exceed industry standards.
Despite our best efforts, no security system is impenetrable. In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected users and regulatory authorities as required by law.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to:
  • Provide our services to you
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements (typically 5-7 years for financial records)
  • Resolve disputes and enforce our agreements
  • Maintain security and prevent fraud
When we no longer need your information, we securely delete or anonymize it. Specific retention periods:
Data Type Retention Period
Account information Duration of account + 5 years after closure
Transaction records 7 years (regulatory requirement)
KYC documents 5 years after account closure
Marketing preferences Until you unsubscribe or close account
Technical logs 90 days (for security purposes)

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

7.1 Access & Portability

  • Right to access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format

7.2 Correction & Deletion

  • Right to rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")

7.3 Objection & Restriction

  • Right to object: Object to processing of your data for certain purposes
  • Right to restriction: Request limitation of data processing

7.4 Marketing Preferences

  • Right to opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time
  • Right to withdraw consent: Withdraw consent for data processing
How to Exercise Your Rights: To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@luminawealthai.com. We will respond within 30 days.
Limitations: Some rights may be limited by legal obligations. For example, we cannot delete transaction records required for regulatory compliance, even if you request erasure.

8. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to:
  • Essential cookies: Required for platform functionality (authentication, security)
  • Performance cookies: Analyze platform usage and improve performance
  • Functionality cookies: Remember your preferences and settings
  • Analytics cookies: Track user behavior for platform optimization
You can control cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling essential cookies may prevent you from using certain platform features.
Do Not Track: We honor "Do Not Track" browser settings and do not track users who have enabled this feature.
